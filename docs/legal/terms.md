## 1. About these terms

This website is run by **[[Steel City Codes' legal name]]** ("SCC", "we", "us"). By creating an account or using the site, you agree to these terms and to our [Privacy Policy](/privacy). If you don't agree, please don't use the site, and contact us at [denver@steelcitycodes.org](mailto:denver@steelcitycodes.org) to register another way.

## 2. Who can have an account

- **Parents and guardians** must be **18 or older** and the parent or legal guardian of every camper they register.
- **Volunteers** must be at least **14**. Applicants under 18 need a parent or guardian's permission, given by their signature on the application.
- **Staff accounts** are given by SCC only.
- Campers don't have accounts.

## 3. Your account

- Give accurate information, and keep it up to date, especially allergies, medical notes and emergency contacts. Camp staff rely on them to keep campers safe.
- Keep your password private. Don't share your account; each guardian or volunteer should have their own. †
- Tell us right away at [denver@steelcitycodes.org](mailto:denver@steelcitycodes.org) if you think someone else has used your account.

## 4. Registration rules

These are the rules the site follows. SCC's program policies have the final say.

- **Spots are first come, first served** from the published opening time. A spot is confirmed as soon as the site shows it as yours. †
- **One class per camper per week.** The site won't let a camper hold two spots in the same week, or join another class's waitlist that week while holding a spot. †
- **Waitlists:** when a spot opens, it's offered to the next camper in line. Campers moved out of a class the camp cancelled go first, then campers without a spot that week, in the order they joined. You have **48 hours** to accept an offer (**12 hours** in the week before camp starts); if you don't, it passes to the next family. †
- **Changes:** you can cancel or switch classes yourself until **14 days before** that session starts. After that, send a change request through the site, and staff will respond. †
- **The camp may change or cancel a class** (for example, if a teacher can't make it). If a class is cancelled, we'll email you, help you choose another class, and put you first in line for that week's waitlists. †
- **Ages and grades:** classes are set by grade and age. The site only offers classes your camper is eligible for, and staff may move a camper whose details turn out to be wrong.

## 5. Volunteers: camper information is confidential

If you volunteer, you'll see information about the campers in your class, including health notes and emergency contacts. You agree to:

- use it **only** to look after those campers during camp;
- not copy, screenshot, share or post it, or take it off the site, except for the printed roster SCC provides for your class, which you return or destroy at the end of camp [[confirm SCC practice]];
- respect each family's photo choice ("No photos" on the roster) †;
- report any problem with camper information to SCC staff right away.

Breaking these rules can end your access to the site and your volunteer role.

## 6. Acceptable use

Don't:
- try to see or change information that isn't yours or your class's, or test the site's security without SCC's written permission;
- use automated tools to copy data from the site or overload it;
- impersonate someone, or register anyone without the right to do so;
- post anything unlawful, harassing or unrelated to camp in updates or messages.

We may suspend or close an account that breaks these terms, or to protect campers, families or the site.

## 7. Emails and messages

By registering, you agree that we may email you about your account, registrations, waitlist offers, changes and camp logistics. You can unsubscribe from **announcement** emails at any time, but registration emails will still come while you have an active registration. † [[If SMS is added later (backlog B1), texts will be sent only to people who opt in.]]

## 8. Availability and changes to the site

We work to keep the site running, especially when registration opens, but we can't promise it will always be available or free of errors. We may change, pause or improve features. If something goes wrong with a registration because of a site error, contact us, and we'll work with you to put it right.

## 9. Responsibility

The site is provided "as is." To the extent the law allows, SCC isn't responsible for losses caused by using or being unable to use the site, beyond correcting the registration itself. Nothing in these terms limits responsibilities that can't be limited by law, and these terms don't change SCC's waiver or program policies.
