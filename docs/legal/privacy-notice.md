## 1. Who we are

This website is run by **[[Steel City Codes' legal name]]** ("SCC", "we", "us"), a nonprofit that teaches kids to code. The Denver chapter uses it to run registration and camp for its summer program. SCC is responsible for the personal information collected here.

**Questions or requests:** [denver@steelcitycodes.org](mailto:denver@steelcitycodes.org) · [[postal address]]

This policy covers the website at [[registration web address]] only. SCC's other activities are covered by SCC's own policies.

## 2. The short version

- We collect what we need to register campers, keep them safe at camp, run our volunteer program, and report on who we serve.
- **We never sell your information, show ads, or use advertising or analytics trackers.**
- Volunteers see only what they need to look after the campers in their own class: names, ages, allergies, medical notes and emergency contacts. †
- You can ask to see, fix or delete your family's information at any time.
- Campers don't use this website. A parent or guardian provides their information.

## 3. What we collect

### Parents and guardians
- **Account:** your name, email address and password. The password is stored only in scrambled (hashed) form; we can't see it.
- **Contact details:** phone number, contact email, and an emergency contact (name, phone, relationship).
- **Agreements:** your typed signature on SCC's waiver and consent forms, the date, and which version you signed. †

### Campers (provided by their parent or guardian)
- **About the camper:** first and last name, grade (entering in the fall) and the age that goes with it, school district and school name, and T-shirt size.
- **Health and safety:** allergies or dietary restrictions, medical conditions, and anything else you tell us we should know.
- **Camp logistics:** whether the camper has a laptop, lunch needs, whether they attended before and which program, and treat (candy) consent.
- **Optional reporting questions:** ethnicity, gender, and free or reduced-price lunch eligibility. These are **optional**, used only for grant and funder reporting in combined form, and never shown to volunteers. †
- **Registration and attendance:** the classes and weeks you sign up for, waitlist status, and roll-call marks at camp (arrival, after lunch and dismissal: present or absent, the time, and any note the class lead adds).
- **Photo and media consent:** whether you allow photos or video of your camper. This is a separate choice, off unless you say yes. †
- [[Camper email address: the current form asks for one. The plan is to remove it (spec L2) unless SCC needs it. If kept, say what it's for here.]]

### Volunteer applicants and volunteers
- **Application:** name, email, phone, age, grade and school, T-shirt size, the weeks you're available, why you want to volunteer, past SCC volunteering, programming languages and classes, experience with children, skill ratings, and course preferences.
- **Signatures:** your typed signature and, if you're under 18, your parent or guardian's.
- **Interview:** the time you book. SCC staff may keep interview notes. Applicants and volunteers can't see these notes.
- **Volunteering:** your experience level, the classes and duty slots you're assigned to, and the roll calls you take.
- [[If SCC records volunteer clearances in the site (spec L7): the clearance type, issue and expiry dates, and who verified it. No copies of background-check documents are stored.]]

### Messages
- Updates posted by SCC staff or a class lead, and records of the emails the site sends you (what was sent and when, not the email's full history). †

### Automatically
- **Sign-in and security logs:** our hosting providers record technical information such as IP address, browser type and the time of each request. We use it to keep the site secure and working.
- **On your device:** the site stores your sign-in session, your light or dark display choice, and which notifications you've already seen, in your browser's local storage. No advertising or tracking cookies are used.
- **Fonts:** the site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when a page loads. [[Remove this line once the fonts are self-hosted (backlog B3).]]

## 4. How we use it

| Purpose | Information used |
|---|---|
| Create and secure your account | Account details, security logs |
| Register campers, run waitlists, and send confirmations and changes | Camper, parent and registration details |
| Keep campers safe at camp | Health and safety, emergency contacts, roll calls |
| Contact families and volunteers about camp | Contact details, updates, emails |
| Recruit, interview and assign volunteers | Volunteer application and assignments |
| Report to funders about who we serve | Optional reporting questions, **combined and without names** |
| Meet legal duties and resolve disputes | Signed agreements and records we must keep |

We don't use your information for advertising, we don't sell it, and we don't build profiles of children or teens.

## 6. Children and teens

**Campers** (about ages 9–14) don't create accounts or use this site. Their parent or guardian provides and manages their information, and must be at least 18.

**Volunteers** can be 14–17. Applicants under 18 need a parent or guardian's signature. We collect only what's needed to run the volunteer program, never use teens' information for advertising or profiling, and don't sell it.

## 7. How long we keep it †

We keep information only as long as we need it, then delete or anonymize it:

| Information | Kept until |
|---|---|
| Allergy and medical notes | 90 days after the camper's last session |
| Camper details (contact, school, optional answers) | 2 years after the camper's last session, then anonymized (we keep only year, grade and class, for reporting) |
| Signed waivers and consents, attendance records | Until the camper turns [[21]], in case they're needed for a legal claim |
| Parent account and contact details | 2 years after your last camper's session, or sooner if you ask |
| Volunteer applications and interview notes | 2 years after you last volunteer (1 year if not accepted) |

Backups of our database are kept for a short time ([[7 days / PITR window]]) and then replaced, so deleted information disappears from them too. [[Align this table with SCC's own records-retention policy, if there is one.]]

## 8. Your choices and rights

- **See or fix your information:** most of it you can view and edit yourself on your dashboard. For anything else, write to us.
- **Delete your information:** you can ask us to delete your family's or your own information. † We'll do it within [[30]] days, except for records we must keep (signed waivers and attendance records, until the date in section 7), which we keep securely and use only for that purpose.
- **Photo consent:** you can change your answer at any time. †
- **Emails:** you can unsubscribe from announcement emails at any time. † We'll still send emails about your registration (confirmations, waitlist offers and changes).

Write to [denver@steelcitycodes.org](mailto:denver@steelcitycodes.org). We may ask you to confirm who you are before acting on a request.

## 9. How we protect it

Information is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored by our database provider with encryption at rest. [[confirm with Supabase]] Access is limited by role: every request is checked by the database itself, not just by the website. Staff accounts are protected with two-step sign-in. † Changes to records are logged, so we can see who changed what and when. †

If a security incident affects your information, we'll tell you as the law requires (in Colorado, within 30 days of determining that a breach happened).

## 10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the version and date above. If the change matters (for example, a new use of your information), we'll tell you by email or on the site, and ask you to agree again where needed.
