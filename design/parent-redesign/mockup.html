Parent pages redesign

A clickable mockup of the parent pages, framed the way most families will see them: in Safari or Chrome on a phone. It adds no features. Every control maps to an RPC or table the app already uses; the work is in where things live and how they read. All names and numbers are made up.

What the research said

From 278 unique threads where a parent wrote in (deduplicated from 1,682 inbox threads; counts only, no names or quotes), specs 10 to 16, 24, 29 to 36 and L6a, and the live demo measured at 375 px.

The pages today

  • The dashboard is 2,814 px tall, 3.5 phone screens. Campers start two screens down, under the week card, the 8-row schedule and three updates.
  • On a phone the top-bar nav is hidden, so Classes is reachable only from a dashboard button.
  • The same class shows twice (week card and camper card), and a waitlisted camper reads "On waitlist" twice on one card.
  • Classes is 2,872 px with a camper picked: every section of every course, both weeks, ineligible ones greyed in place.
  • Fields are 14 px (iOS Safari zooms on focus). 15 of 17 controls on the dashboard are under 44 px.

What parents write in about

  • "Am I in?": 85 threads on confirmation and waitlist status. 58 of them could have been answered on screen, the highest share of any group.
  • When, where, what to bring: 128 threads on dates, times, location, laptops and pickup. 49 could have been answered on screen.
  • Changing a choice: 87 threads on switching or cancelling, and 80 of them needed staff to act. 21 were "we picked the wrong class".
  • Which class fits: 59 threads on level, grade rules and placing with a friend.
  • March to June holds 85% of threads, peaking in May. 2026 alone had 121.

So the home screen answers, in order

  • Anything that needs you: a spot to claim, a draft to finish, details to confirm.
  • Then status per camper per week, in one word.
  • Then when and where: dates, building, drop-off and pickup, from signing up onward, not only in camp week.
  • Then the change path for each class, with the deadline date in words.
  • Details you change once a year (contacts, health) move behind the avatar.

Found in the research, left out on purpose

These would be new features, and some are already specs. Each has an obvious home in this layout, so adding it later won't move anything else.

  • Waitlist position ("2nd in line"): needs a new RPC. Its home is the waitlisted camper row.
  • Laptop loans per week (spec 29): a line in each camper row, and an action in the band when one is needed.
  • Grade and prerequisite rules in the database, and "Request an exception" (spec 31): the folded "Not open to" list on Classes.
  • Move both weeks at once (backlog B10): Switch class inside the camper row.
  • A second guardian on the account (spec 24, households): a section on Family details.
  • Who may pick up, and walk-home consent (spec 36): each camper in Family details, and the camp-day band at dismissal.
  • Urgent notice (spec 34): above the band on Home.
  • Signed forms (L2) and structured allergies (spec 33): each camper in Family details.
