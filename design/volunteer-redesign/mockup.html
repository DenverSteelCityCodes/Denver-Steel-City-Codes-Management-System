Volunteer portal redesign

A clickable mockup of the volunteer pages, framed the way most volunteers will see them: in Safari or Chrome on their phone. It adds no features. Every control maps to an RPC or table the app already uses; the work is in where things live and how they read.

What the research said

From 1,682 inbox threads (66 unique threads where a volunteer or applicant wrote in), the 2026 Master Roster and schedule docs, specs 04, 25, 34–36, B2 and L3, design_system.md, and the live demo measured at 375 px.

The page today

  • The assigned-volunteer page is 5,193 px tall, 6.4 phone screens.
  • Duties start 2.8 screens down and list all 40 slots in one column.
  • Preferences, days off and switch requests sit between the daily tools.
  • Each week's section repeats its own "Roll calls open on…" box.
  • On a phone the name and "Sign out" wrap onto two lines in the top bar.

How volunteers work

  • They think in a week and a class. The Master Roster had a tab per week, and volunteers name themselves by section code ("I'm P2-2").
  • Day-of questions went to Discord and Remind, from phones, mostly after 3 pm. Camp-day tools must work one-handed in a mobile browser.
  • Interview logistics were the biggest cluster: 18 thread-tags for booking, the link, or a missed slot.
  • Availability changes (5 threads) are occasional. They belong in settings, not on the camp-day screen.

Built for the mobile browser

  • Tabs sit at the top. In iOS Safari, a first tap near the bottom edge often just brings Safari's own toolbar back, so a bottom tab bar would take two taps. This replaces the bottom bar suggested in design_system.md §7.6.
  • Every input is 16 px. Below that, iOS Safari zooms the page on focus; today's app uses 14 px fields.
  • The page scrolls normally with sticky headers, uses dvh and safe-area padding, and has no hover-only information.
  • Saves keep the app's existing "check your connection" errors, for weak classroom Wi-Fi.

Found in the research, left out on purpose

These would be new features, and most are already specs. Each one has an obvious home in this layout, so adding it later won't move anything else.

  • Interview join link and onboarding checklist (spec 35): steps two and three of the applicant tracker.
  • Hours and service letter (spec B2): a section on My profile.
  • Co-teachers (the Master Roster's Teachers column): Classes, under the class details.
  • Pickups and "may leave alone" (spec 36) and roll-call notes (spec 34): the Dismissal roll call.
  • Urgent banner (spec 34): above the day header on Today.
  • No-photos flag (L3): next to the health tag on each camper.
  • Resources (orientation recording, packet, setup): an item in the avatar menu.
