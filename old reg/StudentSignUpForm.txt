2026 Denver Steel City Codes Summer
Camp Registration Form
REGISTRATION IS OPEN MARCH 14TH - MAY 11TH (unless capacity is reached earlier).
Registration is on a first-come-first-serve basis. We have a camp capacity of 200 students.
Steel City Codes is a free summer camp for students entering grades 5-9 for the 2025-2026
school year (current 4-8th graders). For students who wish to learn how to code for the first
time or to enhance their existing skills, Steel City Codes is the best option. Our instructors
are experienced high school coders that undergo a rigorous application process, and are
skilled in Python or Java.
The camp will run from 9:30 AM - 3:00 PM each day at the Cherry Creek High School's West
building (9300 E Union Ave, Greenwood Village, CO 80111). A map will be provided.

Steel City Codes Denver is holding the following two sessions for this summer.
- Session 1: June 1st - June 5th
- Session 2: June 8th - June 12th
IMPORTANT: Students will need to pack a lunch EVERYDAY (with the exception of families
financially unable to provide lunch for their students). Students must bring their personal or
school-issued computers.

descriptions):
INTRO TO PYTHON: This introductory course will cover all of the fundamentals of coding
including input/output, loops, logical operations, and more using the very user-friendly
Python language!
Recommended Math Grade Level: 4+

INTRO TO JAVA: This introductory level course will cover all of fundamentals of coding while
diving a little bit more deep into the logic of programming than Intro to Python might.
Recommended Math Grade Level: 5+
INTERMEDIATE PYTHON: This course will cover more material and move at a faster pace
than the Intro to Python course.
Recommended Math Grade Level: 5+ OR Have Taken Intro to Python
INTERMEDIATE JAVA: This course will cover more material and move at a faster pace than
the Intro to Java course.
Recommended Math Grade Level: 6+ OR Have Taken Intro to Java
INTRODUCTION TO MICROCONTROLLERS: This class will be an investigation into the world
of embedded systems programing. We will be using a Raspberry Pi Pico (similar to an
Arduino) and students will work on both class and self guided projects.
This class will be capped at 20 students per week.
REQUIRED Grade Level and Knowledge: The Introduction to Microcontrollers class is only
offered to rising (future!) 7th, 8th, and 9th graders. All prospective students must have at
least an intermediate level of python knowledge.
WEB DEVELOPMENT: This is an introductory course to web development. It will mainly focus
on HTML and CSS programming. This class will be capped at 25 students per week.
Week 2 has reached its cap and has been removed from week 2 class selections.
Recommended Math Grade Level: 6+
