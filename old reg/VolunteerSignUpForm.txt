2026 Denver Steel City Codes Summer
Camp Volunteer Application
Steel City Codes is a free summer camp for students in grades 4-8 for the 2025-2026 school
year. Since we are entirely high school student-led, we rely on dedicated volunteers serving
as teachers and mentors for our students. At the end of the session, we provide official Steel
City Codes volunteer certifications to be used for purposes within high schools.
APPLICATION IS OPEN FROM MARCH 14TH - APRIL 26TH
Steel City Codes DENVER will be holding the following two sessions for this summer.
- Session 1: June 1st - June 5th
- Session 2: June 8th - June 12th
Volunteers will need to stay from roughly 8:30AM to 4:30PM each day, and both of the 2
sessions will be held at Cherry Creek High School.
----------VISIT OUR WEBSITE FOR MORE INFORMATION: https://www.steelcitycodes.org/camps
Email denver@steelcitycodes.org with any questions/concerns.

including input/output, loops, logical operations, and more using the very user-friendly
Python language!
Recommended Math Grade Level: 4+

diving a little bit more deep into the logic of programming than Intro to Python might.
Recommended Math Grade Level: 5+

than the Intro to Python course.
Recommended Math Grade Level: 5+ OR Have Taken Intro to Python
INTERMEDIATE JAVA: This course will cover more material and move at a faster pace than
the Intro to Java course.
Recommended Math Grade Level: 6+ OR Have Taken Intro to Java
